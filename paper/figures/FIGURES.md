- (c): V/Ω ∈ {0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 40} (V = 0 omitted on the log axis; its values are 0.375 and 1); 801 equally spaced times 0 ≤ Ωt ≤ 2π; time average by the trapezoid rule.

Draft caption:

> Two atoms driven resonantly (Δ = 0) from |00⟩ with interaction V = C₆/r⁶. (b) Entanglement entropy of one atom, which stays zero for V = 0 and reaches 0.9994 bits at Ωt = 2.24 ≈ π/√2 for V/Ω = 20, close to the state (|01⟩ + |10⟩)/√2. (c) Time average (circles) and largest sampled value (squares) of the double-excitation probability P₁₁ over 0 ≤ Ωt ≤ 2π, with the V ≫ Ω asymptotes 0.2356(Ω/V)² and (Ω/V)²/2 (grey); the average falls from the free-atom value 3/8 to 9.8 × 10⁻³ at V/Ω = 5 and 1.5 × 10⁻⁴ at V/Ω = 40.

Draft caption:

> Idealised interaction switch-off controls in a six-atom chain (spacing a, V₀/Ω = 5, all pairs V₀(a/r)⁶, Δ = 0, initial state |000000⟩); couplings are set to zero while every atom and its drive are kept, so these are not vacancies. ## fig04_cutoff_error_vs_N

Files: `paper/figures/fig04_cutoff_error_vs_N.png`, `paper/figures/fig04_cutoff_error_vs_N.pdf`

Source data:

- `study/results/maximum_errors.csv` (sha256 `f782246cd4f8`)

Parameters:

- Uniform open chains, N = 4–12; couplings g/|i − j|⁶ with g = V₀/Ω ∈ {2, 8, 16} (R_b/a = 1.12, 1.41, 1.59).
- Models: nearest1 (|i − j| = 1 only), nearest2 (|i − j| ≤ 2), all (every pair, the reference).
- Plotted column: refined_sampled_max_absolute_density_error × 100 (percentage points), the maximum of |n̄_cut − n̄_all| over 81 detunings −3 ≤ Δ/Ω ≤ 7 (spacing 0.125) plus a local grid of spacing 0.0625 within ±0.125 of the coarse maximum. A sampled maximum, not a bound.
- Ground states: dense diagonalization for dimension ≤ 64, otherwise SciPy eigsh (tol 10⁻¹¹); eigenpair residuals ≤ 5.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ (study/results/extension_summary.json).

Draft caption:

> Largest sampled change in the mean excitation density of a uniform open chain when the interaction g/|i − j|⁶ is truncated to nearest neighbours (vermillion) or to index separations ≤ 2 (blue), measured against the all-pairs ground state; filled circles and solid lines are even N, open squares and dashed lines odd N. Each point is the maximum over 81 detunings −3 ≤ Δ/Ω ≤ 7, refined to spacing 0.0625 within ±0.125 of the coarse maximum. Nearest-neighbour truncation shifts the density by up to 8.09 percentage points (N = 11, g = 16, against 4.82 at N = 12); every odd chain exceeds both even neighbours, and at g = 8 and 16 the error still grows with N within each parity up to N = 12, so short even-only sequences understate it. Keeping second neighbours reduces the error to at most 0.19 points; the dimerized-chain calculations therefore keep the long-range tail.

## fig07_solver_scaling

Files: `paper/figures/fig07_solver_scaling.png`, `paper/figures/fig07_solver_scaling.pdf`

Source data:

- `validation/results/scaling_benchmark.json` (sha256 `2e04f374f6bc`)

Parameters:

- Dimerized open chain, gaps 1 ∓ ε alternating (strong first), ε = 0.3, g = 11.39, Δ/Ω = 2.5; even N (one boundary-forced domain wall).
- ED: full 2ᴺ basis, all pairs, rydberg_chain.ed.lowest_states (k = 2) with method 'auto' (eigsh) or 'lobpcg'; wall time = build + solve.
- DMRG: rydberg_chain.dmrg.pair_chain_ground_state, χ = 64, mmax = 8 (inter-pair couplings up to 8 pairs), at most 30 sweeps; the full-range N = 20 run is quoted in the caption only.
- Each case ran in its own subprocess with 2 BLAS threads; memory is that process's peak RSS; machine: Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor @ 2.10GHz, 4 cores, shared.
- Variance of the full-range N = 20 run is −1.0 × 10⁻¹² (rounding) and is not plotted.

Draft caption:

> Cost of exact diagonalization (ED) and pair-site DMRG for an open dimerized chain at ε = 0.3, g = 11.39 (R_b/a = 1.50) and Δ/Ω = 2.5. (a) Wall time, (b) peak resident memory and (c) DMRG energy variance; open symbols mark DMRG runs stopped at the 30-sweep limit before meeting the convergence criteria. At N = 20, DMRG reproduces the ED ground-state energy to 4.2 × 10⁻¹³ with all couplings and to 2.4 × 10⁻⁷ with inter-pair couplings cut beyond 8 pairs, and LOBPCG matches the two lowest eigsh energies to 1.1 × 10⁻¹² with a solve 4.0 to 6.7 times faster. The N = 50 run is close to converged but met neither criterion (last-sweep energy change 1.6 × 10⁻⁹, variance 3.7 × 10⁻⁹); the N = 100 and 200 runs are not converged ground states (variance 1.9 × 10⁻⁴ and 4.3 × 10⁻⁴) and give only the cost per sweep, because the even open chain holds one delocalized domain wall that DMRG moves slowly. Times come from a shared 4-core machine (load average up to 9) and are upper bounds.
