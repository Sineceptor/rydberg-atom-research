## History of corrections

The study's size analysis was corrected from an even-only scan to every N from 4 to 12, and its staggered order diagnostic was corrected for odd N (Q instead of M; see [NOTATION.md](NOTATION.md)). Low-energy states were reinterpreted from configuration probabilities, which revealed the boundary-forced domain wall of even chains. Earlier interpretations that these corrections superseded are not presented as current conclusions.

The 27 September documentation update reframed three parts of the study without changing any data: the interaction-cutoff scan as the reason the long-range tail is kept, the "defect" runs as idealised interaction switch-off controls rather than vacancies, and the kagome patch as an exploratory appendix. Publication figures were redrawn from the saved data in a consistent style.
